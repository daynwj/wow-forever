The roster

The Warlock is the focus. The Shaman comes out when Erik's online, the Mage fills in when a group needs one, and the Druid lives on its own ruleset with Julie's group.

Troll Affliction Warlock

Main · solo first · PvP ruleset

DoTs, drains and Life Tap carry a 45-minute solo session with little downtime, and the Voidwalker holds a second body on the field. No gathering, so no stopping at contested nodes.

Professions & camp

Every character also takes Cooking, First Aid and Fishing. Each profession adds an object to a group campsite; these are the ones this roster brings.

CharacterRulesetProfessionsMakesCamp object
WarlockPvPTailoring + EnchantingRobes, bags, enchantsFaction Banner (Spirit) at skill 20
ShamanPvPHerbalism + AlchemyMana potions, elixirs, flasksIncense Candle (Intellect) at 20; Alchemy Lab from a dungeon Blueprint
MagePvPMining + EngineeringGadgets, bombs, toolsReagent Bot at 20, Repair Bot at 140, Anarchist's Workbench at 300
DruidNormalSkinning + LeatherworkingIts own armor, armor kitsCamp Tent (rested XP) at 20

Camp rules: a Basic Campfire holds three objects, one per player; Cooking upgrades it to five, then ten. Camp buffs don't stack with the class buff they copy, so skip the Incense Candle when a Mage has Arcane Intellect up.

Healing potions moved from Alchemy to First Aid, so everyone makes their own.

How the roster pairs with him

  • Shaman: totems and off-heals cover what a Bear can't, from range. Intellect gear vs his Agility and Stamina, so no fights over drops.
  • Warlock: Fear and Banish add crowd control. Turn off the Voidwalker's taunt first.
  • Mage: the Repair Bot keeps a long session going without a trip to town.
