World of Warcraft: Forever

Four characters across two rulesets, built for a ranged-control, self-sustain playstyle and two very different play windows: solo 45-minute sessions, and co-op whenever Erik's online.

Launch Nov 4, 2026, 3:00 pm PST Beta Sept 17 – Oct 21, 2026 Last reconciled Sept 26, 2026 Source of truth Verification Log, Game Library — Video Games Second Brain (Notion)
PvP Ruleset · Horde

The Erik Camp

Faction-locked ruleset. World PvP is always on in contested zones. Battlegrounds mix in players from other rulesets except Hardcore, but open-world play is Horde-only company here — Erik, his coworkers, maybe his old raid crew.

Troll Affliction Warlock

Main · Solo + Co-op

DoTs, drain healing, and Life Tap keep this character sustained through a 45-minute solo session with almost no downtime. A Voidwalker holds a second body on the field. Affliction doubles as a world-PvP nuisance — hard to kill, hard to ignore.

Race
Troll — Berserking, Rapid Regeneration (50% HP over time, no corpse needed), in-combat Regeneration, Beast Slaying
Professions
Herbalism + Alchemy
Pet
Voidwalker
Talents & leveling notes

Early: Improved Corruption, Improved Life Tap, Suppression (5 ranks, 4% threat reduction / 1% hit per rank), Soul Harvesting.

Around 30: Siphon Life.

Toward 60: Nightfall, Pandemic (up to 33%/rank crit-damage bonus on DoTs), Malevolence, Soul Siphon, Wrack capstone.

Community reports a new 100-yard "Move To" pet command in the beta — lets you position the Voidwalker without relying on Follow/Stay. Unverified whether it survives to launch.

In dungeons with Erik's bear: the Voidwalker has reportedly out-threatened tanks in testing. Turn off its taunt-type abilities in group content.

Skyborne (Windshaper) Druid

Alt · Co-op with Erik

Leans Resto for duo play — off-healing while Erik tanks, Prowl for scouting ahead without spending a talent point on it, flag-running when a battleground calls for speed over sustain.

Race
Skyborne (Windshaper) — Walk on Air, Wind Blessed (+1% haste), Elemental Insight (+5% vs. Elementals), Skysight
Professions
Mining + Engineering
Requires
Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher (not yet purchased)
Spec & healing notes

Rejuvenation, Tranquility, and Wild Growth can all crit in Forever. Bonus healing also adds roughly a third as much bonus spell damage, which helps a hybrid spec more than in Classic.

Prowl is reportedly a trainer spell learned at level 20 — available to any spec, not gated behind Feral talents. Feral Swiftness (the 30% Cat Form speed boost used for flag running) is still Feral-only, so a Resto-leaning Druid scouts fine but runs flags slower than a dedicated Feral would.

Moonkin Form's party crit aura doesn't stack with Leader of the Pack, which Erik's Bear likely carries — one more reason Resto pairs better with him than Balance.

Dual spec has not been announced for Forever. Pick the leveling spec knowing a respec may cost Classic-level gold once the beta's 1-silver price goes away.
Normal Ruleset · Horde (to confirm)

The Julie Camp

Separate economy from the PvP camp — no shared mail, no shared auction house. This character has to be fully self-sufficient. Still open: whether Julie's group is actually Horde. If not, the race below falls back to Skyborne (High Order).

Undead Frost Mage

Solo · Occasional Group with Julie

The most self-sufficient of the four — Cannibalize handles health and mana between pulls with no crafted consumables required. Frost kiting and Shatter combos carry the solo leveling; Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost carry it into battlegrounds.

Race
Undead — Cannibalize (35% HP + mana over time; Blizzard has called this out as especially useful for Mages)
Professions
Tailoring + Enchanting, Fishing
Talents & leveling notes

Ice Lance (11-point talent, 300% damage vs. Frozen targets), Frostbite (15% freeze chance on Chill), a compressed 3-rank Shatter, Fingers of Frost (21 points), reworked Winter's Chill, and a new baseline Frostfire Bolt.

Wands no longer scale with spell damage as of the Sept 24 beta notes — plan gear priorities around spellpower gear, not a wand upgrade.

Whether Cannibalize still requires a nearby corpse in Forever hasn't been confirmed. If Julie's crew turns out to be Alliance, this becomes a Skyborne (High Order) Mage instead — same profession plan, no Cannibalize.

All three of Dayn's characters take Cooking (Well Fed grants +5% experience from kills, on top of stats) and First Aid. No profession is doubled up across characters that can trade — Legacy tradeskill points only come from crafting professions, so redundant gatherers earn nothing.

For Erik: Feral Bear Tank Druid

Why this pairs with the Warlock

  • Threat management matters more than usual — the Warlock's Voidwalker has reportedly out-threatened tanks in early testing. Worth calling out taunt timing before a dungeon run, not after a wipe.
  • Skinning + Leatherworking is the natural profession pairing here, and it's why the Druid alt above took Mining + Engineering instead of doubling up on leather.
  • Fear and Banish from the Warlock cover crowd control the Bear can't provide alone — useful for pulls bigger than the two of you can just tank-and-spank.

Open questions worth checking together

  • Whether every group member can skin the same corpse — Erik's read, not yet confirmed against any source. If true, it changes how much leather actually needs gathering versus buying.
  • Whether War Stomp (if he goes Tauren) or a Troll/Orc racial serves him better as a tank — worth a quick pass once Bear Form's threat tools are confirmed live.
  • Whether he ends up adding a Hunter alt, which would change who's available for dungeon runs on a given night.

Rulesets, plainly

What's confirmed

  • No realms — Normal, PvP, and Roleplaying at launch; Hardcore arrives after launch.
  • PvP locks the whole account to one faction. Normal, RP, and Hardcore allow both factions.
  • No cross-ruleset or cross-faction grouping, trading, or (for grouping and trading) shared economy.
  • Battlegrounds do mix players from different rulesets — except Hardcore.

Still unverified

  • Exact /pvp flagging behavior on the Normal ruleset.
  • Whether gold or items can be mailed between your own characters on opposite factions.
  • Whether auction houses are split by faction within a ruleset.
