Fighting in Bear, 10–19

  1. Mark of the Wild before the pull.
  2. Wrath or Moonfire from range. Wrath’s base damage went up about 50% on Sept 24 and needs no talents ✓ Verified.
  3. Shift to Bear as the mob closes, Enrage for rage (level unchecked ? Unverified), then Maul whenever you have rage.
  4. Demoralizing Roar on tougher mobs; Bash a caster ? Unverified level.
  5. Two or three mobs from 16: Demoralizing Roar, Swipe, Maul the one you want dead first.
  6. After: shift out, Rejuvenation, eat and drink. Don’t shift out mid-fight; you lose rage and pay mana.

Fighting in Cat, 20–39

  1. Prowl in behind the target. Open with Shred (22) or Rake (24); Ravage (32) or Pounce (36) once you have them ◐ Provisional (ability levels from client data).
  2. Build combo points with Claw from the front or Shred from behind. Spend Omen of Clarity procs on the most expensive attack.
  3. Finish with Rip on mobs that live a while, Ferocious Bite on mobs that die fast.
  4. Shifting Power from 25 when Energy runs low.

What changed in Forever

  • Feral attacks scale with weapon damage, so a better weapon is a straight damage upgrade for the cat and Erik’s bear ✓ Verified. That settles the earlier conflict with Classic guides, which said weapon DPS was worthless for ferals.
  • DoTs and drains can crit, unlike Classic ✓ Verified.
  • Crit and hit apply to every attack type, Haste is a new secondary stat, and weapon-skill racials became Expertise ◐ Provisional.
  • Druids use consumables in any form, and Travel Form picks herbs without shifting out ✓ Verified.

Rested XP

  • Rested XP doubles kill XP until it runs out. Classic rules: it builds while logged out in an inn or a capital, about 5% of a level per 8 hours, capped at 1.5 levels; quest turn-ins don’t use it ? Unverified for Forever.
  • That makes levels 1–5 a poor place to park: a full pool at level 1 is about 600 XP, at level 10 about 11,400 (Classic numbers) ? Unverified. Level an alt a little before parking it.
  • The Leatherworking camp tent tops rested up to 5% of a level, with a 1-hour lockout ✓ Verified. The Cozy Sleeping Bag and cooked food stack with it.
  • Dungeon mob XP is heavily reduced; the XP lives in dungeon quests ✓ Verified.

Cozy Sleeping Bag

What it does: lay it out anywhere that isn’t already rested (not in cities or inns). Each minute on it adds a stack of Well-Rested, +1% XP, up to 3 stacks. It also counts as a rested area while you lie there. It stacks with camp buffs and cooked food, so sleep at the campfire and eat at the same time. One-hour cooldown. Anyone in the party can use one bag, so you and Erik can share one. It vanishes if you walk about 100 yards away, and PvP-flagged and unflagged players can’t share one ✓ Verified.

Leveling routes

Cat druid, with Erik
  1. Zephras Isle 1–12 with Erik, including the Horde relic quest at 5 and the Skyborne Bear Form quest at 10.
  2. Airship to Skywatcher Plateau. Thunder Bluff at 12–13: flight path, trainer, Rahauro’s two Ragefire quests, and ask Turak Runetotem about Teleport: Moonglade, which Cat Form at 20 needs.
  3. Eastern swing 13–16, the Silverpine sidetrack: Orgrimmar for Thrall’s and Neeru’s Ragefire quests (grab the Mural of Ta’zo library book in the Valley of Spirits), zeppelin to the Undercity for Varimathras’s quest, the Brill breadcrumb (library book in the alchemy shop), the Silverpine north loop and the Sepulcher flight path (library book on the bottom floor; another in Ambermill). No druid trainer on this side, so train first in Thunder Bluff.
  4. Ruins of Lordaeron 15–17 with Erik (quests from Tabitha Heartweaver at the Sepulcher, Deathguard Kristof, Morbin Lightbane and Theodore Griffs). Ragefire Chasm at 16–17 with all five quests.
  5. Barrens from the Crossroads at 17. Cat Form in Moonglade at 20 with Erik. Wailing Caverns next; Embrace of the Viper gives Cat Form a snake look.

Mulgore to 13–14 is the no-travel fallback. The Sepulcher is not needed for Ragefire or Wailing Caverns. It matters for Ruins of Lordaeron now and Shadowfang Keep later.

Shaman

Mulgore and Thunder Bluff to about 13–14, then the Barrens. Totem quests at 4, 10, 20 and 30. Water Shield at 20 is the point where mana stops being the limit. Camp tent at Leatherworking 20. Dragonscale near 40.

Mage, with Andes Mints

Valley of Trials and Durotar together, Orgrimmar for trainers, then the Barrens. Herb along the route rather than detouring; she’s the only one who stops for nodes. Expert Alchemy at 20 (Undercity), Artisan at 35 (Stonard). Natural flasks need Alchemy 300 ✓ Verified, and their herbs come from level 40–60 zones if the recipe data holds ? Unverified, so the flask tier needs no starter-zone farming.

Undead warlock, PvP

Deathknell, Brill and the Undercity, Silverpine to about 10–12 gathering herbs, then the Barrens and Orgrimmar until Herbalism hits 150 (about levels 12 to 20), then battlegrounds. In Classic, battlegrounds open at level 10 in brackets of ten, and battlemasters stand in Orgrimmar, the Undercity and Thunder Bluff ? Unverified for Forever. She queues from Orgrimmar and banks there. Honor buys the level-60 set, which is no help while leveling.

Troll warlock, Normal

Valley of Trials, then Durotar, with trainers at Razor Hill and Orgrimmar. Voidwalker out front, Drain Life and Life Tap to sustain. Healthstones, Soulstones and summons for the group (Classic spells; levels unchecked in Forever) ◐ Provisional. Free class mount quest at 40.
