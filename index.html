The Erik camp

PvP ruleset, Horde. The whole account is locked to one faction here. Every zone above level 15 is contested and auto-flags you, so anything gathered here is gathered in the open.

Troll Affliction Warlock

Main · solo first, co-op when Erik's on

DoTs, drain healing and Life Tap keep this character going through a 45-minute solo session with almost no downtime. The Voidwalker holds a second body on the field. With no gathering profession there are no stops at contested nodes: cloth drops from the mobs you're already killing, and Enchanting works from disenchanted gear.

Talents and leveling notes

Early: Improved Corruption, Improved Life Tap, Suppression (5 ranks, 4% threat reduction and 1% hit per rank), Soul Harvesting.

Around 30: Siphon Life.

Toward 60: Nightfall, Pandemic, Malevolence, Soul Siphon, Wrack capstone.

A 100-yard "Move To" pet command is community-reported in beta unverified. In dungeons the Voidwalker has reportedly out-threatened tanks, so turn off its taunt with Erik's Bear.

Tailoring's passive, Cloth Scavenging, gives a chance of extra cloth on humanoids community-reported. Robes and enchants made here can gear the Mage later.

Skyborne Elemental Shaman

Duo alt · played with Erik's Feral Bear when you're in range

A ranged caster with totems as the thing on the field, off-heals when the Bear takes a beating, and Ghost Wolf to get out. No stealth, which matters less with a tank beside you. Sturdier than a caster Druid (shield, mail at 40) and no gear competition with Erik: Elemental wants Intellect, his Bear wants Agility and Stamina.

Spec and gear notes

Elemental to level: Lava Burst, Lightning Overload (Lava Burst and your filler can double-cast at half effect with no extra threat or mana).

Resto later: Riptide capstone, Chain Heal bonus. Dual spec hasn't been announced, and the launch respec price isn't published (beta is 1 silver).

Leather until 40, then mail, is the Classic rule Forever unconfirmed. Elemental burns mana fast, so Alchemy's mana potions earn their keep here.

Herbalism and Alchemy only advance on the nights this character gets played. Nothing depends on them, though the Mage can use the mana potions later.

Frost Mage

Bank alt · parked at the auction house · played once the Warlock hits 60

Created early and parked at an auction house to hold and sell for the other PvP-ruleset characters. Later it becomes the battleground character, leveled through battlegrounds, solo play and questing. By then the Warlock can make its robes and enchants and the Shaman its mana potions.

Profession options (open)

A parked level-1 Mage has no professions yet: Apprentice needs level 5. Jewelcrafting and Inscription don't exist in Forever. Herbalism, Alchemy, Tailoring and Enchanting would duplicate the Shaman and Warlock.

  • Mining + Engineering (leading option): Mining adds 5% health community-reported, and its bars feed Engineering. Engineering's battleground gadgets are untested at the current cap unverified.
  • Mining + Blacksmithing (runner-up): the Sharpening Wheel camp buff (Attack Power for an hour, from skill 20) helps Erik's Bear Blizzard-stated. The Mage can't wear what it makes.
  • Engineering + Blacksmithing: covers both crafts nobody else has, but no gathering and no health bonus.
  • Mining + Skinning: two gatherers, nothing crafted. Overlaps Erik's Skinning.
Frost notes

Ice Lance (11-point talent, 300% damage vs Frozen targets), Frostbite (15% freeze chance on Chill), 3-rank Shatter, Fingers of Frost (21 points), reworked Winter's Chill, baseline Frostfire Bolt. Wands no longer scale with spell damage as of the Sept 24 beta notes.

Blizzard with Improved Blizzard, plus Frost Nova freezes, are the AoE tools in the beta guides. A Frost Water Elemental is mentioned by one low-quality site only unverified.

No source confirms experience from battlegrounds; the documented rewards are Marks and Honor unverified. Warsong Gulch opens at level 10.

Reserve the name during the Oct 27 – Nov 3 window.

The Julie camp

Normal ruleset. Separate economy: no shared mail, no shared auction house with the PvP characters. This one has to stand completely on its own. World PvP is opt-in here: you flag yourself manually.

For Erik: Feral Bear tank

Skinning + Leatherworking. Here's how the Shaman fits next to him.

Why the Shaman pairs well

  • Totems and off-heals cover what a solo Bear can't, and the Shaman's damage stays at range while he holds the pull.
  • No gear fights: Intellect caster gear vs his Agility and Stamina leather.
  • Herbing next to a tank is the safest gathering on the PvP ruleset.
  • On Warlock nights: Fear and Banish cover crowd control the Bear can't. Turn off the Voidwalker's taunt first.

Worth checking together

  • Whether every group member can skin the same corpse. Erik's read, no source yet.
  • Whether he adds a Hunter alt, which changes who's around on a given night.
  • Whether Engineering gadgets work in battlegrounds, if the Mage takes it and once the cap allows testing.

Rulesets, plainly

Confirmed

  • No realms. Normal, PvP and Roleplaying at launch; Hardcore after launch.
  • PvP locks the account to one faction. Normal, RP and Hardcore allow both.
  • No cross-ruleset or cross-faction grouping or trading.
  • Battlegrounds mix players from different rulesets, except Hardcore.
  • No shared mob tagging.
  • On Normal, RP and Hardcore, world PvP needs manual flagging.

Logged, not yet confirmed

  • On the PvP ruleset, zones above level 15 are contested and auto-flag you.
  • Mailing gold or items to your own opposite-faction characters.
  • Whether auction houses split by faction within a ruleset.
