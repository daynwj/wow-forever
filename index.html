How to play him while leveling

The Voidwalker tanks. Open with Searing Pain once Demonic Brand is in: it brands the target so the demon’s next hits deal extra damage, and it makes Searing Pain cost less threat. Then DoTs and Shadow Bolt. Out of combat, Life Tap when health is higher than mana, and get the health back with Drain Life or food. From 30, Soul Link keeps him alive; keep the demon healthy, and recast Soul Link after summoning a new one ? Unverified. Fel Domination is the fast resummon when the Voidwalker dies.

Sacrificing in dungeons

Before Demonic Pact (leveling): don’t sacrifice. The sacrifice bonus is cancelled when any demon is summoned, and Soul Link needs a demon out ✓ Verified. With a real tank, put out the Imp (Blood Pact Stamina for the group) or the Succubus (the most demon damage).

At 60 with the Fire build: sacrifice the Succubus, then summon the Imp; Demonic Pact keeps the bonus while the Imp is out. Fire is the dungeon pick because Hellfire and Rain of Fire get every bonus. Never resummon the demon you sacrificed: that cancels the bonus. On packs of 3 or more, Hellfire once the tank has them, or Rain of Fire from range. Bane of Agony on each mob as the pack comes in; Curse of the Elements on the main target ✓ Verified.

The other level 60 option is Tavern’s Shadow build (sacrifice the Imp, keep the Succubus out). Its players switch to Fire for long AoE runs by sacrificing the Succubus and summoning the Imp, and switch back the same way ✓ Verified.

Level 60 single-target priority (Fire)

Searing Pain when the brand is missing; your Bane (Doom with a minute or more left, Agony after); your Curse; Immolate; Soul Fire while Decimation is up; Corruption; Life Tap in gaps or while moving or when the Imp runs low; Searing Pain as the filler. Use Blood Fury and other cooldowns in the 15 seconds before a Doom explodes ✓ Verified.

Why this shape

Levels 10–50 are pure Enhancement, because the Enhancement tree is exactly 51 points and every point spent elsewhere delays Improved Ghost Wolf. The talent needs 15 Enhancement points, so rank 1 comes at 25 at the earliest and instant Ghost Wolf at 26; taking Stormstrike first makes it 26 and 27. Ghost Wolf itself is learned at 20 with a slow base cast. The last 10 points go to Restoration (cheaper heals, a little hit, mana regeneration while casting); a full Restoration second spec at 40 is the real dungeon-healing answer.

Blizzard says Strength becomes Enhancement’s best stat after its Nov 10 tuning (not posted yet). The Intellect-based talents (Mental Dexterity, Mental Quickness, Ancestral Knowledge) may lose value; Ancestral Knowledge is the only one that can be swapped out freely. {PROV}

Why these professions

Skinners gather from the kills already in front of them, so neither the druid nor the shaman breaks off to chase nodes while leveling. Mojita is the one who runs to herbs. What it costs: the raiding druid gives up Mixology (own elixirs and flasks last twice as long and hit 25% harder; potions are not covered) ◐ Provisional. Mojita carries Alchemy with no cap; Cerulean Dye is an Alchemy-only intermediate.
