Fighting in Bear, 10–19

  1. Mark of the Wild before the pull.
  2. Wrath or Moonfire from range. Wrath’s base damage went up about 50% on Sept 24 and needs no talents ✓ Verified.
  3. Shift to Bear as the mob closes; with Furor from 15 you start with 10 Rage. Maul whenever you have rage.
  4. Demoralizing Roar on tougher mobs; Bash a caster ? Unverified level.
  5. Two or three mobs from 16: Demoralizing Roar, Swipe, Maul the one you want dead first.
  6. After the fight: shift out, Rejuvenation, eat and drink.

Fighting in Cat, 20–39

  1. Prowl in behind the target. Open with Shred or Rake; Ravage (32) or Pounce (36) once you have them ◐ Provisional.
  2. Build combo points with Claw from the front or Shred from behind. Spend Omen of Clarity procs on the most expensive attack.
  3. Finish with Rip on mobs that live a while, Ferocious Bite on mobs that die fast.
  4. No Shifting Power until 40. Furor covers the gap: a heal break no longer costs your Energy.

PvP: warlocks and hunters

  • Shifting forms frees you from snares (Travel Form confirmed, Cat and Bear reported) ◐ Provisional. Hibernate takes a pet out of the fight.
  • Warlock DoTs and pets break Prowl. Open on the player from behind, not the pet.

Cozy Sleeping Bag

What it does: lay it out anywhere that isn’t already rested (not in cities or inns). Each minute on it adds a stack of Well-Rested, +1% XP, up to 3 stacks. It also counts as a rested area while you lie there. It stacks with camp buffs and cooked food, so sleep at the campfire and eat at the same time. One-hour cooldown.
