Fighting in Bear, 10–19

  1. Mark of the Wild before the pull.
  2. Wrath or Moonfire from range. Wrath’s base damage went up about 50% on Sept 24 and needs no talents ✓ Verified.
  3. Shift to Bear as the mob closes, Enrage for rage (level unchecked ? Unverified), then Maul whenever you have rage.
  4. Demoralizing Roar on tougher mobs; Bash a caster ? Unverified level.
  5. Two or three mobs from 16: Demoralizing Roar, Swipe, Maul the one you want dead first.
  6. After: shift out, Rejuvenation, eat and drink. Don’t shift out mid-fight; you lose rage and pay mana.

Fighting in Cat, 20–39

  1. Prowl in behind the target. Open with Shred (22) or Rake (24); Ravage (32) or Pounce (36) once you have them ◐ Provisional (ability levels from client data).
  2. Build combo points with Claw from the front or Shred from behind. Spend Omen of Clarity procs on the most expensive attack.
  3. Finish with Rip on mobs that live a while, Ferocious Bite on mobs that die fast.
  4. Shifting Power from 25 when Energy runs low.

With Shift Happens tanking

  • Let Erik pull and give him two or three seconds of threat. Bear Form’s threat bonus is 50% in the Oct 8 beta notes ◐ Provisional.
  • Prowl in behind, Shred or Rake to open, Rip on long fights, Bite to finish.

PvP: warlocks and hunters

  • Berserk at 40 clears and blocks Fear for 15 seconds. Save it for a warlock’s Fear ✓ Verified.
  • Hunters can’t stun or interrupt and have an 8-yard dead zone; stay inside it with Feral Charge or Dash ✓ Verified.
  • Shifting forms frees you from snares (Travel Form confirmed, Cat and Bear reported) ◐ Provisional. Hibernate takes a pet out of the fight.
  • Warlock DoTs and pets break Prowl. Open on the player from behind, not the pet.

What changed in Forever

  • Feral attacks scale with weapon damage, so a better weapon is a straight damage upgrade for the cat and Erik’s bear ✓ Verified. That settles the earlier conflict with Classic guides, which said weapon DPS was worthless for ferals.
  • DoTs and drains can crit, unlike Classic ✓ Verified.
  • Crit and hit apply to every attack type, Haste is a new secondary stat, and weapon-skill racials became Expertise ◐ Provisional.
  • Druids use consumables in any form, and Travel Form picks herbs without shifting out ✓ Verified.

Warlock head start: skins and bags for the shaman

The Undead warlock is created days before the shaman, so she can stock his Leatherworking before he exists.

  1. Day 2–3: train Skinning and Tailoring, not Enchanting. Skin every beast in Tirisfal and Silverpine, and keep the linen from humanoids. The druid mails her linen from Zephras Isle.
  2. Level 10: Journeyman Tailoring needs level 10 and skill 50, which is where wool bags and wool gear start (rank gates checked on other crafts; Tailoring assumed the same) ? Unverified. Make her own cloth gear, then a stack of linen and wool bags.
  3. Undercity bank: leather, hides and bags sit there until the shaman is created. Mail them, plus her Skinning Knife, on his first night.
  4. When the shaman starts: she unlearns Skinning and trains Enchanting. Nothing is lost: Enchanting needs green items to disenchant and level 10 for Journeyman anyway.

Why it’s worth it: the shaman can start Leatherworking at skill 1 with materials in hand, making leather gear from his first levels and reaching the camp tent (rested XP) at Leatherworking 20 within a session or two ✓ Verified for when the tent opens. Cozy Sleeping Bag

What it does: lay it out anywhere that isn’t already rested (not in cities or inns). Each minute on it adds a stack of Well-Rested, +1% XP, up to 3 stacks. It also counts as a rested area while you lie there. It stacks with camp buffs and cooked food, so sleep at the campfire and eat at the same time. One-hour cooldown. Anyone in the party can use one bag, so you and Erik can share. It vanishes if you walk about 100 yards away, and PvP-flagged and unflagged players can’t share one ✓ Verified.

Horde route, level 14 or higher ✓ Verified:

  1. The Barrens: Burned-Out Remains, a ruined tower at 46, 74 in the south. Starts “…and that note you found”.
  2. Westfall: Nailed Plank at the Alexston Farmstead, 37, 50. Rewards a Swiftness Potion and a Lesser Troll’s Blood Potion. Starts Stepping Stones. Alliance zone.
  3. Stonetalon Mountains: a path in the hills near Sun Rock Retreat leads to a camp with Pocket Litter (Flint and Tinder, Simple Wood, Sturdy Lunchbox, a Mining Pick or Farmer’s Shovel). Light the nearby campfire for the optional Rekindle. Then Scramble: a Mound of Dirt just east. That pays 3 Student Fodder, 200 Feathered Arrows, 200 Exploding Shot, and a choice of Silver Star, Moonsight Rifle or Precision Bow. Starts Wet Job.
  4. Loch Modan: Carved Figurine on a ledge of the Stonewrought Dam, 49, 12. The hardest step for Horde: long trip, level-30 guards nearby. Starts Eagle’s Fist.
  5. Hillsbrad Foothills: climb the cart leaning on the gap in the northern Thoradin Wall, follow the top of the wall to the Messenger Bag, then click the Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel under it. Cozy Sleeping Bag plus 5 Student Fodder.

Student Fodder fully heals you and restores some mana; it no longer gives XP in Forever. Two of the steps are in Alliance zones on the PvP ruleset, so run the chain with Erik.
