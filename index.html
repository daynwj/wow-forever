Options I'm still weighing

Druid: Feral from 10, or Balance to 16 and swap?

Feral from 10 (what this page assumes). No respec fee, and Wrath works without talents since its Sept 24 buff ✓ Verified. Swipe arrives at 16 ✓ Verified, so 16–19 is the farm-feral-gear window either way. Most guides level Feral and Bear from 10 to 19 ✓ Verified.

Balance to 16, then swap buys little (a few Wrath talents) and pays a respec at a launch price nobody has published; the beta's 1 silver is temporary. ✓ Verified beta-only

Mage: straight to Orgrimmar, or level first?

Level to 5–6 first (recommended). Gets apprentice engineering at 5 and the camping quest, and conjure water and food in Classic's levels. A level 1 bubble is nearly worthless.

Straight to the city only wins if the walk is the point. It skips quests that give early skill and costs the same trip later.

Mage and warlock: Razor Hill inn or Orgrimmar?

Razor Hill keeps the hearth bound near the unfinished zone, but has no auction house or bank.

Orgrimmar (recommended): the services are right there, both alts share one city, and Razor Hill is a short run south.

Park at 5–6 or push to 10?

10 brings the voidwalker, journeyman ranks and a bubble worth about 19 times a level 1 bubble. It also costs more leveling before the alt can sit. The mage has no pet to wait for, so there's less reason to rush.

Start at 5–6; push the mage and warlock to 10 once the druid is settled.

Shaman: Resto early for mana, or Elemental first?

Elemental first (the table in Talents). Convection, Elemental Alacrity and Call of Thunder come early; Water Shield lands at 37.

Resto first gets Water Shield at level 20 but delays the damage talents. Elemental stays mana-starved ✓ Verified, so test both in the beta. ⚑ Needs test

Swap rules

  1. Play an alt when its rested bar is full and it has kill-objective quests waiting. Do those first, then deliver the quests.
  2. The alt that's parked in a capital is the bank alt for that stretch. Mail materials across when you swap.

  4. Don't spend rested XP in dungeons. Dungeon mob XP is heavily reduced, and the extra dungeon quest XP was cut by half on Oct 1. ✓ Verified
